About

A small library with a long memory.

Waraq started in 2019 as two shelves in a bookshop on Gamet El Dowal. It is still small on purpose: every title is read before it goes up, and every copy is chosen for the edition, not the bestseller list.

Questions people ask

Do I need to pay to borrow?

No. Borrowing is free with a membership, and membership costs nothing. You only pay for books you decide to keep.

What happens if a book comes back late?

Nothing dramatic. We hold your borrowing for a week and ask you to bring it back. We would rather have the book than a fine.

Can I order a book you don't have?

Usually yes. Send us the title through the form below and we will tell you the price and how long it takes.

Are the covers on this site the real editions?

They are photographs of the editions we stock, taken from free archives like Wikimedia Commons. Credits are in the footer and the repository.
